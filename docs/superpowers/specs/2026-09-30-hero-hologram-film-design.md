# Hero Hologram Film — "The Commission"

**Date:** 2026-09-30
**Status:** Approved by owner 2026-10-01. Supersedes `2026-07-23-hero-scrub-film-design.md` ("The Atelier Commission") and its plan, which was never started.
**Scope:** A ~38 s muted autoplay loop in the homepage hero that sells NexxVantage as a custom software and AI studio; the keyframe and clip production pipeline (Gemini → Kling → Resolve); and the hero component change that plays it.

Concept board (opening motion): https://claude.ai/artifact/534SbVsk7byyiWCnknCnB9 — Concept 1 "The Core Ignites".
Character board: https://claude.ai/artifact/FC2dYresHim2uTLxyBsuMk — robot R4 "The Halo".

## 1. Goal and decisions

A visitor should feel, without reading a word: *these people are ahead in AI, every app they make is built around the client, and their clients are delighted.* The film must look like the hero itself coming alive, not like a video placed on the page.

| Decision | Choice |
|---|---|
| Story | "The Commission": one client, brief → live build → approval. Plus one beat showing an AI agent inside the built app, and the stacked screens standing for past clients' apps. |
| Opening | Concept 1, "The Core Ignites": a gold point draws the NexxVantage mark, gold light rays project holographic screens (desktop **and** mobile) that burst out and orbit the mark. |
| Assistant | Robot R4 "The Halo": glossy pearl-white sphere, dark navy visor with two gold dot eyes and a small gold smile, a thin tilted gold ring orbiting it, two floating sphere hands. Solid, not holographic — it is the real agent. Rounded everywhere; no sharp or boxy forms. |
| Client | Photoreal woman, mid-40s, midnight-navy silk hijab with a woven gold border, projected as a gold hologram: fine scanlines, translucent edges, shoulders dissolving into gold sparkles, sparkles close to the body only (no swirl). Reference file: §6. AI-generated, nobody's likeness. |
| Look | Gold (`#C9A84C`) and warm white light on midnight. No cyan or blue light (brand palette). Screens are gold-edged glass panels with scanlines. |
| Frame | Square 1:1 in the hero's right column (existing grid), stacked under the text on mobile. **The whole frame is flat `#0F1E35`** — no floor, horizon, shadow, vignette or gradient — so the square's edge is invisible. |
| Delivery | Muted autoplay `<video>`, `loop`, `playsinline`, about 38 s. Ends on the gold point it starts from, so the loop has no seam. |
| Sound | None in the hero (browsers only autoplay muted). A voiced cut for social/YouTube is a later, separate job reusing these visuals. |
| Words in film | Only "Approved". It is added in Resolve in Space Grotesk, never generated by Kling. No statistics or testimonials in the film; real proof lives in the HTML below the hero ("Transparent by Default"). |
| Production | Stills: Gemini (Nano Banana). Clips: Kling image-to-video with **start and end frame**, so each clip's last frame is the next clip's first and the joined film is one continuous shot. Post: DaVinci Resolve (free) + ffmpeg. |

## 2. Beat sheet

Nine keyframe stills, K0–K8, define the film. Each generated clip runs from one still to the next. K8 is the same image as K1, so the last move (mark → point) is **clip 1 played in reverse**: no generation, and the loop closes exactly.

| # | Time | Clip | Start → end still | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–5 s | Ignition | K0 → K1 | A single gold point pulses in the flat field, then draws the NexxVantage mark in light, line by line. |
| 2 | 5–10 s | Projection | K1 → K2 | Fine gold light rays shoot from the mark's core; at their ends holographic screens form — 3 desktop, 3 mobile — then burst outward and settle into a slow orbit around the mark. |
| 3 | 10–15 s | Arrival | K2 → K3 | The robot floats in from the right, reaches into the orbit with one sphere hand and draws a **mobile** screen out of it to hover at its side. |
| 4 | 15–20 s | The call | K3 → K4 | The robot taps the mobile screen twice. It ripples and unfolds into the client hologram, head and shoulders, talking to the robot. |
| 5 | 20–25 s | Stack | K4 → K5 | The robot gestures at the orbit: one **desktop** screen comes forward, the rest slide into a layered stack behind it. The camera moves: the mark to the left, still projecting rays into the stack at centre; the robot to the right at mid-height (never a corner); the client floating above-right, turned toward the robot. |
| 6 | 25–30 s | Build | K5 → K6 | The robot works the front screen with small hand gestures. It starts as a gold wireframe and fills into the client's finished app, ending with an AI chat panel sliding in on its right side. The client leans in and points. |
| 7 | 30–35 s | Approved | K6 → K7 | The client nods and smiles, then dissolves into gold sparkles that gather into a large gold tick in her place. "Approved" fades in beneath it (post). |
| 8 | 35–40 s | Return | K7 → K8 | The robot gives a small wave. Tick, stack, screens and robot fold back along the rays into the mark; the camera returns to centre. |
| 9 | 40–43 s | Close | K8 → K0 | Clip 1 reversed and sped up: the mark collapses to the gold point. |

Raw length is about 43 s; Resolve speed-ramps the calm stretches (orbit, stack) to land near 38 s. Durations above are generation lengths, not final timing.

**Layout of K5–K7 in the square** (fixed once in K5, reused in K6 and K7 so nothing jumps): mark centred at about (22%, 55%); front screen of the stack at about (50%, 45%), roughly 36% of the frame wide; robot at about (80%, 60%); client at about (76%, 24%). The stack's rays originate at the mark's core.

**Mobile legibility.** The square is 280 px on phones. Screen content will be texture there, not readable; the story carries through shape and motion (robot, face, tick). The mark, the client's smile and the tick are the three things that must read at 280 px, and K5–K7 are checked at that size before any clip is generated.

## 3. Screen content

The screens are **realistic software designed for the film**, not the owner's real screenshots and not abstract gold lines. Each is laid out like a real, working product (navigation, plausible data, tables, charts, an AI assistant taking action) and rendered as a gold hologram on glass. Business names and figures are invented, so no screen claims to be a real client and no client work is exposed. Real full-colour screenshots belong in the portfolio section, not the film.

Style sample (approved 2026-10-01; the reference for every screen): https://claude.ai/artifact/TxiNC45L3pEWcCRiKPT1nL

| Screen | Form | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel operations ("Seabreeze OS") | Desktop | KPI row (occupancy, arrivals, ADR, RevPAR), room-booking timeline, AI Concierge panel proposing and approving an upgrade. **The app built in clips 6–7.** |
| Hotel operations, wireframe state | Desktop | Same layout as gold wireframe outlines only (start of the Build beat, K5) |
| Legal case management | Desktop | Case table with status and deadlines, document viewer, AI document-review panel |
| Retail analytics | Desktop | Sales chart, inventory alerts, AI demand forecast |
| Hotel guest app | Mobile | Stay card with digital key, AI concierge chat |
| Shopping app | Mobile | Product cards, AI recommendations |
| Incoming call | Mobile | Caller ring and accept control; the screen the robot taps in clip 4 |

The AI Concierge avatar inside the apps is a miniature of R4, tying the product to the assistant in the film.

**How screens enter the film:** each is built as HTML/CSS in the brand style (as in the sample), rendered to a high-resolution PNG, and attached to the Gemini keyframe prompts so the panels carry exactly this design. Resolve free has no planar tracker, so screens are never tracked on in post. At 440 px desktop and 280 px mobile the detail reads as texture; what must survive is the silhouette of real software (sidebar, KPI row, timeline bars, chat bubbles).

## 4. Hero integration

`HeroMovement.tsx` keeps its left column (overline, H1, sub, CTAs) unchanged. The right-column square's `MarkStatic` + `MarkCanvas` pair is replaced by one new component.

- `src/components/hero-film/HeroFilm.tsx`: a `<video muted loop playsInline autoPlay preload="none" poster aria-hidden>` filling the existing `280px` / `440px` square. Sources: `hero-film.av1.mp4` (AV1), then `hero-film.h264.mp4` (fallback for Safari and older devices). The video is decorative; the hero's text carries the meaning.
- **Poster** = K0 rendered (gold point on midnight), a few KB, so first paint is almost the empty hero and the video's first frame matches it.
- **Loading:** the `src` is attached only after the page has loaded and the square is on screen, so the video never competes with the hero text for LCP. It pauses when scrolled out of view and resumes when back.
- **Fallbacks** show a still instead of the video: `prefers-reduced-motion`, `navigator.connection.saveData`, or autoplay refused (`play()` rejects, e.g. iOS Low Power Mode). The still is K6 (the finished app with robot and client), since it tells the story on its own; the gold-point poster would look empty. No-JS is not a separate case: the hero text already needs JS to fade in, and without JS the `<video>` simply shows its poster.
- **Edge insurance:** a soft CSS `mask-image` fade on the outer ~5% of the square. The frame is already flat `#0F1E35`, but video codecs can shift a dark flat colour by a step or two; the fade makes any shift invisible. Nothing important is placed in that outer band.
- `MarkCanvas`, `MarkScene`, `useCan3D` stay: `MethodCTA.tsx` still uses `MarkCanvas`. Only the imports leave `HeroMovement.tsx`.

**Why `<video>` now, when the previous spec banned it:** the ban was for scroll-scrubbing (seeking a video on every scroll event janks). An autoplaying loop never seeks. The colour-shift concern is handled by encoding with explicit BT.709 tags and checking the decoded background (§5).

**Performance gates:** AV1 file ≤ 3 MB, H.264 ≤ 6 MB, 1080×1080, 30 fps. Poster ≤ 20 KB. No layout shift (the square's size is fixed before load). Lighthouse LCP unchanged from today's hero.

## 5. Production pipeline

Film assets live outside the repo at `~/NexxVantage-film/` (existing convention); only the final encoded files and posters go into `public/hero-film/`.

| Step | Tool | Output | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 References | Gemini + HTML | R4 robot design sheet (front, three-quarter, side); client reference (done); mark reference from `Logo.tsx`; the seven designed screens from §3 rendered to PNG | Owner approves the robot sheet and the screens |
| 2 Keyframes | Gemini, **edit mode**, always deriving from an approved still with references attached, never generating from scratch | K0–K7 (K8 = K1), 1:1, ≥ 1024² | All eight approved, checked at 280 px, **before any Kling credit is spent** |
| 3 Flatten | Resolve (or ffmpeg) | Every keyframe's background brought to exactly `#0F1E35` | Background samples within ±1 of `#0F1E35` |
| 4 Clips | Kling image-to-video, start and end frame, 1080p, 5 s, sound off, professional mode | 8 clips (clip 9 is reversed clip 1) | Best of up to 3 takes per clip |
| 5 Edit | Resolve | Conform, speed-ramp to ~38 s, background flatten, "Approved" title, loop check (last frame → first frame) | Owner approves the cut |
| 6 Encode | ffmpeg | AV1 and H.264 MP4, BT.709 tagged; K0 and K6 posters | Decoded background checked in Chrome and Safari; size gates in §4 |

**Credits:** 8 clips × 5 s is the same 320-credit pass the earlier plan budgeted. Allow two to three passes for retakes.

**Consistency rules** (the main risk is the robot and the client changing between clips):
- Every still that shows the robot is made with the R4 design sheet attached; every still with the client has the client reference attached.
- Clips are generated in order and each clip's actual last frame is exported and checked against the next keyframe; if Kling drifted, the next clip starts from the drifted frame (the chain is what makes it seamless, not the original still).
- Motion prompts ask for slow, smooth movement. Fast moves are where Kling distorts faces and hands.

**Global style block** (prepended to every still and clip prompt):
> Futuristic holographic scene, premium cinematic render. Everything floats in empty space against a completely flat, solid, uniform dark navy background (#0F1E35) filling the entire frame edge to edge — no floor, no surface, no horizon, no cast shadow, no vignette, no gradient, no haze. Holograms are warm gold (#C9A84C) and soft white light with fine horizontal scanlines and glowing gold edges; no blue or cyan light. Square composition with a generous empty margin at the edges. No text, no logos, no watermarks.

The per-keyframe and per-clip prompts are written in the implementation plan, one per step.
